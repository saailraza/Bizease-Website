FAQ

Do you work with startups?

Yes, from early-stage startups to established enterprises.

Can Pakistani residents open companies abroad?

In most of these jurisdictions yes, subject to licensing, KYC and (in some cases) local sponsor or office requirements. We confirm per case.

How long does incorporation take?

Varies by country and activity: from days (United Kingdom, United States) to several weeks (Saudi Arabia licensing).

Can you handle multiple services?
